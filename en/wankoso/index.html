01 / What is Wankoso?

Thoughtfully made.
Simply served.

WANKOSO brings fermented plant ingredients into a convenient liquid format for dogs. Kidney Support combines fermented plant extract with selected botanicals, fruit ingredients, oligosaccharides, lactic acid bacteria and chicken powder.

Open a sachet, add the appropriate amount to your dog's usual food, and make it part of a familiar feeding routine.

03 / Your sample

A small sachet.
A simple first step.

Your sample contains one 10 mL sachet of Kidney Support. Use it to explore the aroma, texture and your dog's acceptance.

  1. Choose the amount for your dog's body weight using the daily feeding guide.
  2. Mix into your dog's usual food.
  3. Observe how your dog responds and share your feedback with us.
The sample is an introduction to the product, rather than a full trial of a daily routine. One sample sachet may contain less than the daily guide amount for larger dogs.

Follow the original package instructions for storage, expiry and handling after opening. Keep the sachet and batch information. Please email us if you need help reading the Japanese label.

A few useful answers.

What is the difference between the sample and the retail product?

The sample contains one 10 mL sachet of Kidney Support for an initial introduction. Contact us for retail pack specifications and availability.

Is this a treatment for kidney disease?

No. WANKOSO is a food supplement for dogs. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent disease and does not replace veterinary care or a prescribed diet.

Does “lactic acid bacteria” mean live probiotics?

The ingredient name alone does not establish live probiotic content. Please request the product specification if you need details about processing or bacterial ingredients.

Can you send more detailed product information?

Yes. Email us with your name, company, country or market, and what you would like to know. We can discuss product specifications, distribution and OEM or co-development.
